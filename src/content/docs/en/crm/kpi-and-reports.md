CRM has three ways to measure results: **Reports** to analyse deals and activities, the **Revenue Forecast** for what you expect to close, and **Sales Org & Goals** for monthly targets.

## Reports

Go to **CRM** › **Selling** › **Reports**.

1. Choose **Report on**: deals or activities.
2. Choose **View by**, such as owner, stage or source.
3. Choose **Measure**: count or value.
4. Set the date range.
5. Click **Export CSV** to use the data elsewhere.

## Revenue forecast

The **Revenue Forecast** page works out a weighted total:

Forecast = deal value × the win % of the deal's stage

Deals without a value or an expected close date aren't counted, so fill in both on every deal. Use the deal's **Forecast category** (Pipeline / Best case / Commit) in team discussions.

## Sales Org & Goals

Go to **Settings** › **Sales Org & Goals**.

1. Click **Add to sales team** and set **Reports to** to build the team tree.
2. Set monthly targets for each person:
   - **Won target**
   - **New-deals target**
   - **Activity target**

Managers see everyone in their branch. Reps see their own target in the **My goal this month** card on the home page.

## KPIs on the CRM home

The home page shows win rate, won this month, overdue tasks and a 6-month trend. Field visit KPIs are on the Team tab of the [Sales Plan](/en/docs/crm/visit-plans-and-field-sales).

## KPIs and performance reviews

To tie sales results to yearly reviews, use **Performance** in the People module, which has review cycles, company KPIs, scorecards and calibration. See [Employees](/en/docs/people/employees).

## Tips for accurate reports

- Set realistic win % per stage in **Pipelines**.
- Close lost deals as **Lost** rather than leaving them open.
- Fill in **Lead source** on every deal to see which channels work best.
