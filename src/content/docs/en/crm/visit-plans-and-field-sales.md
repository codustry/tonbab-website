The CRM **Sales Plan** helps reps plan who to call or visit and when, and shows managers how much field work actually happens. Field sales visits are in every plan, Free included.

## Sales Plan tabs

Go to **CRM** › **Customers & visits** › **Sales Plan**. It has 4 tabs:

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| List | Tasks grouped into **Overdue**, **Today**, **This week** and **Recently closed** |
| Day route | The day's route; check in and finish visits |
| Team | Field team overview for managers |
| Calendar | All activities in a calendar view |

## Plan your touches

- Click **Plan activity**, choose call, visit or email, and set a date.
- Click **Add cadence** for a repeating rhythm, such as a call every 14 days. Closing a task restarts the count from that touch.
- Close an activity with **Done** or **Skip**.

## Visit a customer

1. On the Day route tab, click **Add visit**, then use **Best order** to put the nearest stop first and **Open full route** in maps.
2. At the customer, tap **I've arrived**. Tonbab records the time and GPS position. If the customer has no pin yet, you're asked to pin your current position.
3. Tap **Finish visit** and fill in the **Outcome**, **What happened**, **Photo (optional)** and **Next step** with a date.
4. If the visit didn't happen, choose **Couldn't visit** and give a reason.

## Day report

At the end of the day, write the **Day report**, then **Save draft** or **Submit to manager**. You can't submit while visits are still open. The manager clicks **Acknowledge** once read.

For a full day in the field, use **Request work away** so attendance counts you as present.

## For managers: the Team tab

The **Team** tab shows **Today in the field** and **Field KPIs** per rep: visits, completion, check-ins, productive visits, far check-ins, reports and follow-ups.

A check-in more than 300 m from the customer's pin is flagged for the manager. It's a warning only, not a block.

## Visit outcome settings

Under **Settings** › **Visit outcomes**, click **Add outcome**, enter English and Thai names, and tick **Productive** for outcomes that count as a successful visit. These count toward the team's productive-visit rate.

## Sales team and reporting lines

Set up the team under **Sales Org & Goals**: click **Add to sales team** and set **Reports to**. Managers see everyone in their branch. Anyone not placed in the tree sees only their own records. For targets, see [Reports and goals](/en/docs/crm/kpi-and-reports).
