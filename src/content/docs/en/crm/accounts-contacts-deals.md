Tonbab CRM is built for B2B selling. Business accounts use the same company records you deliver to and invoice, so deals, contacts and delivery history sit on one page.

## Accounts and contacts

Go to **CRM** › **Customers & visits** › **Customers**. The page has 3 tabs:

- **Accounts**: click **New account**. Filters are **All**, **Has open deals**, **Touch planned** and **Untouched**.
- **Contacts**: click **New contact**. One person can hold several roles, such as purchasing or decision maker.
- **Map**: customer pins coloured **Visited ≤ 30 days**, **Not visited 30+ days** and **Never visited**. Use **Near me**, **Add to today**, **Plan visit tomorrow** and **Navigate**.

Pin a customer's location by opening their account while on site, or by pasting a Google Maps link.

### The account page

Each account shows **Open pipeline**, **Won total**, **Cadence** and **Next touch**, with sections for **Planned touches**, **Deliveries** and **Quotations in Accounting**. Click **Add deal** to open a deal for this account.

## Deals and pipelines

Go to **Selling** › **Deals**.

- View as **Board** or **Table**, and keep views under **Saved views**.
- Drag a card to change stage, or use **Deal actions** › **Move to stage** / **Move to pipeline**.
- Deal fields: **Deal title**, **Value (฿)**, **Expected close**, **Win probability %**, **Lead source** and **Forecast category** (Pipeline / Best case / Commit).
- Close a deal as **Won** or **Lost**, and **Reopen** it if needed.

The CRM home lists **Stale deals — no movement in 14+ days** to follow up.

### Pipeline settings

Under **Settings** › **Pipelines**, click **New pipeline** (it starts with Lead, Contacted, Quotation, Negotiation and Won/Lost), then **Add stage** as needed. Each stage has a **Win %**, a number of days before a deal goes stale, and an **Auto-task on entry**. Deal tags are set under **Deal Tags**.

## Quotation lines on a deal

The **Quotation lines** section on a deal works out its value:

1. Click **Add line**, pick a product (optional), and enter **Qty**, **Unit price (฿)** and **Line discount**.
2. Add a **Quotation discount** and choose the **VAT mode**.
3. Click **Print quotation** for a draft to discuss with the customer.

### Discount approval

An admin sets the threshold in **Sales Org & Goals** › **Approvals** (**Require approval when discount ≥ (%)**). A larger discount sends a request to the rep's manager. While it waits, the deal can't change stage or close.

## Numbered quotations

Official quotations are issued only by Accounting, so there is one quotation numbering series. On the deal, click **Create numbered quotation**. Tonbab opens an accounting quotation linked to the deal, and its status (awaiting customer, accepted, declined) shows back on the deal. See [Sales documents](/en/docs/accounting/sales-documents) (Accounting is included from the Starter plan up).

## From deal to job

When you win the work, click **Create Job Control** to track budget and cost. See [Job control](/en/docs/operation/job-control-costing).

## Plan limits

CRM customers: Free 200, Starter 1,000, Standard 5,000, Business and up unlimited. Open deals: Free 50, Starter 200, Standard 1,000, Business and up unlimited.
