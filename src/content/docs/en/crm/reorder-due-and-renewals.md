Tonbab CRM helps you look after existing customers in two ways. The **Who to call — reorder due** list works from real delivery history, and **Renewals** tracks service contracts and recurring fees.

## Reorder-due customers

The CRM home has a **Who to call — reorder due** list.

### How it works

Tonbab looks at each customer's delivery notes and works out roughly how often they order. If today is past that rhythm, the customer appears in the list with a note such as "Orders every ~30 days — now 45 days since the last delivery".

It works without any setup, as long as you have delivery notes in Tonbab (created there or imported).

### What to do next

- **Plan today** adds the customer to today's calls or visits.
- **Log touch** records that you've been in contact.

### Other home cards

- **My today**: today's tasks
- **Calls to make**
- **Pipeline pulse**
- **My goal this month**

## Renewals

Go to **CRM** › **Selling** › **Renewals**.

### Create a contract

1. Click **New contract**.
2. Pick the customer, value and **Billing cycle** (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annual or Custom).
3. Enter an **Annual uplift** if the price rises each cycle.
4. Tick **Renews automatically** if the contract rolls over by itself.

### Overview

The top shows active contracts, **Due within 60 days**, **At risk** and **Renewal rate**.

### When a cycle comes due

- **Renew** closes the current cycle and opens the next.
- **Create deal** works the renewal as a deal in the pipeline (useful when terms need renegotiating).
- Update the payment status: Unpaid, Invoiced, Paid or Waived.

### Contract statuses

Active, Renewed, Not renewing, Lost or Cancelled. If a customer doesn't renew, pick a reason such as price, competitor, budget cut, contact left, project ended or service quality, for later analysis.

## Tips

- Import past delivery notes so the reorder-due list is accurate from day one.
- Set a regular cadence for key customers in the [Sales Plan](/en/docs/crm/visit-plans-and-field-sales).
