The **Government tenders** menu pulls procurement notices from the Comptroller General's e-GP system automatically, based on your keywords and the agencies you watch. Reps can move promising notices into the pipeline in one click. This feature is in the Business plan and up.

## How notices come in

- Tonbab pulls notices from the agencies you watch twice a day, at 07:35 and 20:35 Bangkok time.
- When new notices match your keywords, users get an in-app notification.
- **Refresh now** pulls immediately, but e-GP only serves its feeds roughly between 17:00 and 09:30. Outside those hours you won't get anything new.

## Set keywords and watched agencies

Go to **Government tenders** › **Keywords & watched agencies**.

1. Add keywords to match against project titles. Matching looks for the term anywhere in the title, so very short terms bring in a lot of noise. Generic Thai wording works better than brand names.
2. Add **Exclude terms** for words you never want.
3. Click **Find buyers for my keywords**. Tonbab ranks agencies that already buy in your category.
4. Click **Watch** on each agency, or **Watch selected**, or search the e-GP agency directory.
5. Optionally turn on an email digest when new matches arrive.

## Triage notices

The tenders page has 4 tabs:

| Tab | Meaning |
|---|---|
| New | Notices not yet triaged |
| In pipeline | Notices added as deals |
| Passed | Notices you decided not to pursue |
| Past awards | Already-awarded contracts, for market intelligence, not open tenders |

For each notice:

- **Add to pipeline** creates a deal in the tender pipeline (shortcut A).
- **Pass**, with a reason such as not our product, budget too small, too far, not enough time or incumbent locked in (shortcut X).
- **Open notice on e-GP** to see the original documents.

## After adding a deal

Tender deals work like any other deal, with **Tender** as the lead source. Move stages, add quotation lines and follow up in [Deals](/en/docs/crm/accounts-contacts-deals).

## If no notices arrive

- Check that you watch at least one agency.
- Try broader keywords.
- If you see a message that the API isn't configured, contact the Tonbab team.
