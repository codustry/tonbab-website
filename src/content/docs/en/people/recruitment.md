The **Recruitment** menu covers everything from posting a job to a new hire's first day. Candidates fill in their application, pick an interview time, accept the offer and submit onboarding documents through their own link. This feature is in the Business plan and up.

## Your careers page

1. Go to **Company Settings** › **Careers page**.
2. Fill in about the company, benefits, HR email and phone, LINE ID and Google Maps link (Markdown supported).
3. Turn on **Publish the public careers page**.

The careers page lists every open position. Its address (slug) is set by the Tonbab team.

## Job postings

1. Go to **Recruitment** › **Job Postings** and click **New posting**.
2. Enter the position, description and requirements.
3. Publish it. Each posting has a **Public link** to share on social media or job boards.

Posting status: Draft, Published and Closed.

## Track applicants

Under **Applicants**, each person sits in a stage:

Invited › Applied › Screening › Interview › Offer › Hired (or Rejected / Withdrawn)

- Change stage with **Move to**.
- Invite a candidate you already know to fill in an application.
- Each applicant has their own tracking link.

## Schedule interviews

1. On the applicant page, under **Interview scheduling**, click **Add slot** for each free time.
2. The candidate picks a slot through their link. It then shows as booked.
3. See all interviews under **Interviews**.

## Offers and contracts

1. Click **Issue an offer**, enter base salary, probation length and salary, raise after probation and start date, then send it.
2. If the candidate accepts verbally or on paper, you can record the acceptance for them. They move to Hired.
3. For the contract, click **Load from template**, then **Send contract** for the candidate to accept online, or **Upload signed PDF**.

Create templates under **Settings** › **Contract Templates**. Contracts can be sent once the candidate has completed the full application form.

## Onboarding documents

The candidate submits bank details and onboarding documents on their own **Onboarding documents** page, so HR doesn't need to collect them by email.

## Convert to employee

Click **Convert to employee** (needs an accepted contract or the Hired stage). Tonbab creates an employee with an employee code, using the salary, start date and position from the accepted offer.

- Salary is copied only if the person converting has the salary permission.
- Bank details and national ID are copied only with the sensitive-data permission.

Then continue in [Employees](/en/docs/people/employees) and [Payroll](/en/docs/people/payroll).
