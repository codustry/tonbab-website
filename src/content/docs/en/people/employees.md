The People module keeps every employee's details in one place, from name, position and department to statutory data and pay. Sensitive data has its own permissions, so only the people who need it can see it.

## Employees don't have to be users

Employees in People and users of the system are separate. An employee who never signs in to Tonbab can still have a record, take leave and be paid. Employees you can record: Free 10, Starter 10, Standard 50, Business 200, Enterprise unlimited.

## Add an employee

1. Go to **People** › **Directory** and click **Add employee** (or **Import from users** to create from existing users).
2. Enter names in both languages:
   - **First name (Thai)** and last name in Thai
   - first and last name in English
   - Thai and English nicknames
3. **Display name** fills itself in if left blank. **Employee code** is generated if left blank.
4. Choose **Gender** (used for some leave types), department, position and manager.
5. Turn off **Eligible for overtime** for managers with authority over others (Labour Protection Act, section 65).
6. Click **Create employee**.

## The employee page

Each employee has **Profile**, **Organization**, **Attendance**, **Documents**, **Salary history** and **All requests** tabs. People only see the tabs they have permission for.

Employee status: Active, On leave or Terminated. Former employees and **External collaborators** have their own lists.

## Permission tiers

| Tier | Data | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| General | Name, position, department, contact details | People with permission to view employees |
| Sensitive | **Thai statutory** (national ID, tax ID, social security number, dependents, provident fund), **Banking** (including PromptPay) and the Documents tab | People with the sensitive-data permission |
| Salary | **Compensation** (pay type, rate, frequency), salary history and every payroll page | People with the salary permission (admins and HR) |

Managers who approve leave or attendance but hold neither of the upper tiers only see their own reporting line. Set permissions in [Users, roles and permissions](/en/docs/getting-started/users-roles-permissions).

## Departments and org chart

- **Departments**: click **Add**, **Rename** or **Deactivate**. A department that still has employees can't be removed.
- **Org chart**: view by **Hierarchy** or **By department**. It's built from each employee's manager field.

## Change requests

Employees can update some personal details from **My profile**. Changes that need checking go to **Change requests** for HR to approve.

## Each employee's own pages

Under **My work**, every employee has **My profile**, **My time & leave**, **My expenses**, **My payslips**, **My tax allowances (ล.ย.01)** and **Goals**.

## Performance and KPIs

**Performance** for HR has review cycles, company KPIs, scorecards and calibration. Start with **New cycle**, then follow the usual rhythm: set targets, check in mid-year, review at year end.
