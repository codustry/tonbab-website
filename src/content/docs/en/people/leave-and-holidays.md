Tonbab sets up leave types that follow Thai labour law for every company from the start. Staff request leave from their phone, managers approve, and Tonbab deducts the balance automatically.

## Default leave types

| Type | Default entitlement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Annual leave | 6 days after 12 months; carries over | On |
| Sick leave | 30 days, paid | On |
| Personal leave | 3 days | On |
| Maternity leave | 120 days, 60 paid | On |
| Parental leave | 15 days | On |
| Unpaid leave | No set limit | On |
| Military, ordination, training | Per company policy | Off, can be switched on |

Change entitlements or add types under **Settings** › **Leave types**. Check them against your company policy and legal advice before going live.

## Requesting leave

1. Go to **My time & leave** and click **Request leave**.
2. Pick the leave type and the from and to dates.
3. For half a day, choose **Morning (AM)** or **Afternoon (PM)**.
4. Add a reason and an attachment (such as a medical certificate), then submit.

Balances are shown under **My balances**.

## Approving leave

Managers and HR go to **Manage** › **Leave**, tab **Requests** › **Pending approvals**, and click **Approve** or **Reject**. Leave requests also appear on the **Approvals** page with other approvals.

## Leave settings

The **Settings** tab on the leave page has:

- **Run accrual** to add leave entitlements
- **Encash unused leave** for annual leave not taken
- **Public holidays**: add one at a time or in bulk, one per line as `YYYY-MM-DD | English | Thai`
- a team leave calendar with a subscribe link for Outlook, Teams or Google Calendar
- a year summary

## Comp time

If staff work on a rest day, your company can let them take a day off instead of overtime pay.

1. Turn it on in **Company Settings** › **Compensatory time off** › **Allow "exchange for a day off"** (off by default).
2. When an employee requests overtime for rest-day work, they choose **Take as**: **Overtime pay** or **A day off (comp time)**.
3. Banked comp days expire at year end. HR can extend them for an individual.

## Work away

For a full day working outside the office, such as visiting customers, request **Work away**. The day counts as present without using leave.

## Team calendar

**Calendar** shows everyone's leave, holidays and birthdays. Click **Add event** to add a company event (it doesn't count as a work day).

## Leave and payroll

Unpaid leave and leave encashment are picked up in that month's [payroll](/en/docs/people/payroll).
