Common questions customers ask about Tonbab, with links to the related guides. If your question isn't here, contact sales@codustry.com or 02-873-4448.

### What is Tonbab?

Tonbab (ต้นแบบ) is a cloud ERP for Thai businesses. It brings purchasing, stock, production, point of sale, CRM, HR and payroll, service jobs, company docs and accounting into one system, in Thai and English.

### Can I try it free?

Yes. Sign up at [app.tonbab.com/signup](https://app.tonbab.com/signup). New companies get a 30-day Business trial with no card, then move to Free automatically. See [Sign up](/en/docs/getting-started/sign-up-and-company).

### How much does it cost?

Free ฿0, Starter ฿2,990/year, Standard ฿9,900/year, Business ฿39,900/year and Enterprise from ฿120,000/year (before 7% VAT). Monthly billing costs a tenth of the yearly price. See [Plans and billing](/en/docs/getting-started/plans-and-billing).

### How many users does the Free plan include?

One. Starter has 5, Standard 10, Business 25 and Enterprise 100+. Staff who sign in with a PIN on a POS or pack station device don't count as users.

### Can I use accounting right away?

Yes, on every plan, **Free included**. Plans differ by size (users, storage, documents), not by features. See [Accounting overview](/en/docs/accounting/accounting-overview).

### Can I move my data from my current software?

Yes. Move from FlowAccount, PEAK, Express, Xero, QuickBooks and other accounting software via Excel, or have our team do it with the ฿9,900 onboarding service. See [Data migration](/en/docs/migration/migrate-from-other-software).

### Does it connect to Shopee and Lazada?

Yes. Order sync and ship notifications are live. Stock push-back to the marketplaces is in development, and TikTok Shop and LINE Shopping are coming soon. See [Sales channels](/en/docs/commerce/sales-channels).

### Can I answer customer chats from Tonbab?

Yes, for LINE OA, Facebook Messenger and Instagram. Shopee and Lazada chat aren't supported. See [Chat inbox](/en/docs/commerce/chat-inbox).

### Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Open it in the browser and add it to the home screen on iPhone, iPad and Android. Tablets can also run POS or the pack station in kiosk mode. See [Install the app](/en/docs/getting-started/install-app).

### Which Thai taxes does it support?

Tax invoices, PP30, withholding tax with 50 tawi, PND3/53 (in Accounting), and PND1, PND1K and SPS 1-10 (in Payroll). See [Thai tax and labour compliance](/en/docs/thai-compliance/thai-tax-and-labour-compliance).

### Can one login use several companies?

Yes. A user can belong to several companies and switch from the sidebar. Running several companies under one contract is part of Enterprise. See [Switch companies](/en/docs/getting-started/switch-language-and-companies).

### Can it connect to other systems or AI?

Yes, through the REST API and the AI connector (MCP) for Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini CLI. Writes through the API are drafts or requests that a person approves; nothing is posted or paid. See [API and MCP](/en/docs/api/api-and-mcp-quickstart).

### Does Tonbab have AI features? What are AI credits?

Yes. **AI Count** in the pack station counts items from a photo: AI puts a dot on each item, and staff check and correct the dots before saving. AI features use your company's monthly AI credits. Most AI actions use 1 credit and heavier ones use more; AI Count uses 1–2 credits per photo however many items it finds. Allowances are Free 30, Starter 300, Standard 1,000, Business 3,000 and Enterprise 10,000 a month. See [AI credits](/en/docs/getting-started/plans-and-billing#ai-credits).

### Who can see employee salaries?

Only people with the salary permission, usually admins and HR. Sensitive data such as national ID and bank details has its own permission too. See [Users and permissions](/en/docs/getting-started/users-roles-permissions).

### What happens to my data if I stop paying?

When a paid plan ends, it keeps working for 7 days while the renewal is paid. After that the company moves to the Free plan. Your data stays, and you can export master data and stock reports to Excel under **Data Import/Export**.
