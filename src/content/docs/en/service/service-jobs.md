The Service module handles after-sales work end to end: taking a machine in, inspecting, estimating, issuing parts, repairing and handing back, plus scheduled maintenance plans and machines you rent out or lend.

## Open a new job

1. Go to **Service** › **New job**.
2. Pick the customer and machine, and choose **Workshop** or **On site**.
3. Pick a job template if you have one (it brings a checklist and default quote lines).
4. Save the job.

## Job stages

Received › Inspect › Estimate & quote › Parts › Repair › Hand over › Closed

Each job has Overview, **Work**, **Estimate & billing** and Activity tabs. Along the way you can:

- record the machine coming in and going out, with photos at intake, after repair and on return
- add time and expenses, such as labour and travel
- **Issue to job** to take parts from stock, or create a purchase request when parts must be bought
- **Complete the repair**, which needs a failure code and warns about open checklist items or missing parts
- close the job

## Estimates and quotations

Service makes a **cost estimate**, and sales issues the quotation.

1. On the Estimate & billing tab, estimate parts and labour, then click **Send to sales**.
2. Sales opens the CRM deal and clicks **Create numbered quotation**, which is issued through Accounting.
3. Once the customer's answer is recorded, the job moves on by itself. If the customer declines, you can charge the inspection fee you've set.

You can revise an estimate; Tonbab keeps revisions 1, 2 and so on. Service quotes are in every plan, Free included.

## My jobs (technician view)

**My jobs** shows today's on-site visits with **Directions**, **Call** and **Open job**, plus open jobs and the next 7 days.

## Maintenance plans

1. Go to **Maintenance plans**, create a plan, set it to repeat every n months (1–60) and set the next due date.
2. When it's due, click **Open job now**. Jobs are never created automatically.
3. Completing the job moves the next due date forward.

## Rentals and loans

Go to **Rentals & loans** and create a new one. Choose:

- **Free loan**: a handover slip only, never invoiced (for example a loaner during a repair)
- **Charged rental**: charged per day, week or month; sales issues the quotation

Steps: Draft › **Reserve** › Hand over › **Take back** › Closed. Along the way you can record a deposit received, settle the deposit, raise an invoice and add charges on return, such as extra days or damage. (Deposits and invoices need Accounting turned on.)

## Settings and reports

- **Settings**: job number prefix, labour and travel rates, quote validity, warranty, inspection fee, and a deposit before ordering imported parts
- **Job templates**: checklists and default quote lines
- **Failure codes**: code, name and category
- **Reports**: turnaround, first-time fix rate, repeat repairs, top failure causes, by technician, and warranty cost

Labour and travel cost from service jobs can roll into a [job control](/en/docs/operation/job-control-costing). Jobs per month: Free 20, Starter 50, Standard 200, Business and up unlimited.
