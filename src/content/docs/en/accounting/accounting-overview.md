Tonbab Accounting covers Thai bookkeeping from the chart of accounts and sales and purchase documents to withholding tax, VAT, financial statements and year-end close.

> **Plans:** Accounting is included in every paid plan, from Starter up. The Free plan doesn't include it; a new company's 30-day Business trial does. Pick Accounting when you sign up; to add it to an existing company, contact sales@codustry.com or 02-873-4448. The starter chart of accounts and depreciation rates are drafts, so have your auditor review them before relying on them.

## First-time setup

1. **Chart of accounts**: click **Load Thai starter chart**, **Add account** yourself, or import from Excel under **Import**.
2. **Posting accounts**: choose which account each automatic entry uses, such as receivables, output VAT and input VAT.
3. **Fiscal periods**: click **New fiscal year** › **Create year** to create 12 monthly periods. The first year starts on the **Company registration date** entered at sign-up.
4. **Document settings**: choose the document colour, upload the company stamp and signature, or tick **Our company is registered without a seal**.
5. **Opening balances**: import a trial balance as of the day before you start. See [Move your data from your current software](/en/docs/migration/migrate-from-other-software).

## Signatures on documents

Each person uploads their own signature under **My profile**. The company signature is a fallback when the approver has none. Signatures and the stamp print only on issued documents, never on drafts.

## Accounting permissions

- view accounting
- record and post (including preparing payments and creating vouchers)
- manage settings and imports
- issue own quotations, for sales staff. Quotations that need approval show an **Approve and issue** button to those who can approve.
