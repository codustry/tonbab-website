The **Year-end close** page checks you're ready to close the fiscal year, posts the closing entry and locks every period of the year. It also holds your audited figures and a checklist of the year's filings.

> **Plans:** Accounting is included from the Starter plan up. See [Accounting overview](/en/docs/accounting/accounting-overview).

## Before you close

1. **Post depreciation**: under **Depreciation**, click **Post depreciation for {month}**. Missed months are caught up. The period must be open.
2. **Post stock**: under **Stock posting**, choose accounts, click **Create draft voucher**, then post it from the voucher page (after month end, one per month).
3. **Reconcile every bank account**. See [Bank reconciliation](/en/docs/accounting/bank-reconciliation).
4. **Check the trial balance**, receivables and payables under **Receivables / payables ageing**.

## Pre-close checklist

The year-end page checks that:

- the trial balance balances
- there are no draft vouchers dated in the year
- fiscal periods cover the whole year
- a retained earnings account is set
- earlier years are closed

## Close the year

1. Go to **Books** › **Year-end close**.
2. Click **Close {name}**, then confirm with **Close the year**.
3. Tonbab posts a closing voucher, moves profit or loss into retained earnings, and locks every period of the year.

To make changes, click **Reopen the year**. The closing voucher is reversed and only the last period is unlocked.

## Year-end statements

The **Year-end statements** link opens that year's page:

- **Audited figures**: enter the figures from the audited statements. They must match the books before the year can close.
- annual general meeting details

## Financial statements report

**Financial statements** has **Income statement** and **Statement of financial position** tabs, with **Export CSV**. Figures come from posted vouchers and are management figures; the statements you file still go through your auditor.

Other reports: **Trial balance** and **General ledger**.

## Lock periods during the year

You don't have to wait for year end. Lock each month under **Fiscal periods** › **Lock** after its PP30 is filed, and **Reopen** if needed.

## Fixed assets

The **Fixed assets** register has **Add asset** and **Print register**. When you sell or dispose of an asset, click **Dispose** after posting depreciation up to the month before. (Issue a tax invoice separately for VAT on the sale.) Depreciation rates follow Royal Decree 145 and are drafts your auditor should review.
