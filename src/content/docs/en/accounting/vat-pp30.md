The **Tax filings** page gathers the figures for your PP30 VAT return from the month's sales documents and purchase bills, with output and input VAT reports. The VAT tabs only appear for VAT-registered companies.

> **Plans:** Accounting is included from the Starter plan up. See [Accounting overview](/en/docs/accounting/accounting-overview).

## Open the PP30 page

1. Go to **Finance and tax** › **Tax filings**.
2. Choose the month with **Previous month** / **Next month**.
3. Open the **ภ.พ.30 (VAT)** tab.

## The figures

- **Sales** and output VAT, from tax invoices issued in the month
- **Purchases with claimable VAT** and input VAT, from bills whose **Claim in month** is this month
- The result: **VAT to pay** or **VAT credit to carry forward**

A credit carried from an earlier month isn't included. Add it on the PP30 form yourself.

## Tax reports

- **Output VAT report**
- **Input VAT report**

Download each as CSV (UTF-8) to open in Excel. These are figures to prepare the filing, not a file to upload to the Revenue Department directly.

## Mark as filed

After filing on the Revenue Department's system, come back and click **Mark as filed**, entering the filing date, amount paid and receipt reference. If documents change afterwards, Tonbab warns you. If you marked it by mistake, click **Undo**.

PP30 is due by the 15th of the following month, with 8 more days for online filing.

## PP36 for foreign services

If you pay for services from an overseas provider (such as software or online ads), enter the PP36 base when paying, then see the totals on the **ภ.พ.36** tab of the tax filings page.

## Lock the period after filing

After filing PP30, lock that month under **Fiscal periods** › **Lock** so nobody changes documents behind figures you've already filed. If you must change something, use **Reopen**.

## Check before filing

1. Every tax invoice in the month is issued, with no drafts left.
2. Bills with tax invoices have the right claim month.
3. Credit and debit notes for the month are issued.
4. Compare with the output and input VAT accounts in the **Trial balance**.
