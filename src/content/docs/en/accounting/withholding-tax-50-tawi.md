In Tonbab you withhold tax when you record a payment to a vendor. Tonbab issues the withholding tax certificate (50 tawi) with its number and gathers the items into each month's PND3, PND53 or PND54 attachment.

> **Early access:** Accounting is available to invited companies only. See [Accounting overview](/en/docs/accounting/accounting-overview). Employee 50 tawi certificates from payroll are covered in [Payroll](/en/docs/people/payroll).

## Withhold tax when paying

1. Pay the bill under **Payments** or **Prepare payments**.
2. Under **Tax withheld from the vendor**, enter:
   - **On amount**: the tax base
   - **Filed on**: **ภ.ง.ด.3 (individuals)**, **ภ.ง.ด.53 (companies)** or **ภ.ง.ด.54 (paid abroad)**
   - **Type of income paid**, such as service, contract, professional, commission, rent, advertising, transport, insurance, royalty, interest, dividend, prize, or **Other (type your own)**
3. Post the payment.

The vendor needs a tax ID and address, or posting is blocked, with an **Edit vendor** button to fill them in.

## Print the 50 tawi

After posting, the certificate is numbered automatically. Click **Withholding tax certificate (50 ทวิ)** to print it or save a PDF for the vendor.

## See all withholding

The **Withholding tax** menu lists every payment that withheld tax. To withhold on something you pay on the spot, click **Create withholding tax**, which opens a new bill set to be paid immediately.

## Monthly filing

Go to **Tax filings**, pick the month and open the **ภ.ง.ด.3**, **ภ.ง.ด.53** or **ภ.ง.ด.54** tab.

- View the attachment for that form and download the CSV.
- File on the Revenue Department's system.
- Come back and click **Mark as filed**, entering the filing date, amount paid and receipt reference.

The deadline is the 7th of the following month, with 8 more days for online filing. The CSV gives you the figures to prepare the filing; it isn't a file to upload to the Revenue Department directly. If documents change after you mark a form filed, Tonbab warns you.

## Tax customers withheld from you

When a customer withholds tax from a payment to you, enter it on the receipt under **Tax withheld by the customer**. The total shows in the **Tax withheld from you** card on the tax filings page, for your corporate income tax filing.

## Tips

- Complete vendor details before paying, especially tax ID and branch.
- Lock the accounting period after filing so the figures don't change. See [Year-end close](/en/docs/accounting/year-end-close).
