**Docs** is your company wiki inside Tonbab. Use it for working manuals, SOPs, policies and notes. Several people can edit at once, you control who can view or edit, and pages show up in the same search as everything else in Tonbab.

## Spaces

Docs are organised into spaces, such as a sales space or an SOP space.

1. An admin goes to **Spaces & sharing** and creates a space.
2. Choose who sees it: **Everyone in the company** or **Members only**.
3. Set the default access: can view, can comment, can edit or full access.

A space that still has pages can't be deleted.

## Create and edit pages

1. In a space, click **New page**, or add a sub-page under an existing page.
2. Add an icon and cover if you like.
3. Pick a template under **Start with**, or start blank. Any page you reuse often can become a template.
4. Type. Changes save automatically.

While editing you can see who else has the page open, leave comments, and use **Version history** to go back. You can also lock, move, duplicate, export to Markdown, print or save as PDF, and import content.

### Verify a page

For SOPs and policies, use **Verify page…** to set an owner and an expiry date, so the team knows the content is still trusted or due for review.

## Share a page

Click **Share** on the page.

- Add people or roles, or use **As space** to follow the space's access. Sub-pages inherit from their parent.
- **Publish to web**: click **Publish** to create a link anyone can read without signing in. Choose whether to include sub-pages and when the link expires (every plan).

An admin can turn off web publishing for the whole company.

## Databases

Add a database to a page (every plan), empty or from a template such as a task tracker or content calendar.

- Views: Table, Board, List, Gallery and Calendar.
- Property types: text, number, select, multi-select, date, person, checkbox, URL, email, phone, relation, rollup, formula and a link to an ERP record.
- Filters and sorts are saved per person; editors can save them for everyone.

## Search, favourites and trash

- **Search** looks inside every page you can access, or use Tonbab's main search (⌘K).
- Click the star to add a page to your favourites.
- Deleted pages go to **Trash**. Click **Restore** to bring one back, or **Delete forever** if you're sure.

## Pages per plan

Free 200 pages, Starter 500, Standard 2,000, Business and up unlimited.

## Ideas

- Per-position SOPs that new staff can read but not edit
- Company policies verified every year
