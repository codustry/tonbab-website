Tonbab was built for Thai businesses from the start, covering documents, tax and labour law. This page summarises which module handles each topic and when filings are due, so you can see it all in one place.

> Tonbab prepares the figures, documents and files for filing, but the actual filing happens on the Revenue Department and Social Security Office systems. Always have your accountant or auditor review before filing.

## Company details

At sign-up, Tonbab records your **Tax ID (13 digits)**, branch (head office or a 5-digit branch number), VAT registration and **Company registration date**. These print on documents and start your first fiscal year. See [Sign up](/en/docs/getting-started/sign-up-and-company).

## Sales and purchase taxes

| Topic | Where | Due |
|---|---|---|
| Tax invoices, credit and debit notes | [Sales documents](/en/docs/accounting/sales-documents) | - |
| PP30 output and input VAT reports | [PP30](/en/docs/accounting/vat-pp30) | 15th of the next month (+8 days online) |
| PP36 for foreign services | [PP30](/en/docs/accounting/vat-pp30) | Per Revenue Department schedule |
| Withholding tax, 50 tawi, PND3, PND53, PND54 | [Withholding tax](/en/docs/accounting/withholding-tax-50-tawi) | 7th of the next month (+8 days online) |
| Tax customers withheld from you | Receipts and a card on the tax filings page | Supports your PND50 |
| PND50, PND51, DBD e-Filing, บอจ.5 | Checklist in [Year-end close](/en/docs/accounting/year-end-close) | Annual deadlines |

These are in Accounting, which is included in **every plan**, Free included. Electronic tax invoices (e-Tax Invoice) aren't supported yet.

## Payroll and social security

| Topic | Where | Due |
|---|---|---|
| PND1 (ภ.ง.ด.1) | RD file (.txt) and CSV in [Payroll](/en/docs/people/payroll) | 7th of the next month (15th online) |
| SPS 1-10 (สปส.1-10) | CSV in [Payroll](/en/docs/people/payroll) | 15th of the next month |
| PND1K (ภ.ง.ด.1ก) | RD file (.txt) and CSV | Yearly |
| Employee 50 tawi | Printed per employee | Yearly |
| ล.ย.01 tax allowances | Staff fill in **My tax allowances (ล.ย.01)** | - |

Social security is 5% from both employee and employer on wages from ฿1,650 to ฿17,500 (the cap from 2026). Tonbab applies the cap for each year automatically.

## Labour law

| Topic | In Tonbab |
|---|---|
| Statutory leave | Default leave types such as 30 days' sick leave and 120 days' maternity leave (60 paid). See [Leave](/en/docs/people/leave-and-holidays). |
| Public holidays | Add one at a time or in bulk in leave settings |
| Overtime | 1.5× / 2× / 3×, and no overtime for managers under section 65. See [Time and attendance](/en/docs/people/time-attendance). |
| Comp time | Staff can take a day off instead of overtime pay |
| Final pay | Severance, unused leave and pay in lieu of notice, including section 119 cases. See [Payroll](/en/docs/people/payroll). |
| Attendance corrections | The original scan and the reason for any edit are always kept |

## Personal data

Sensitive employee data, such as national ID, bank details and salary, has its own view permissions so only those who need it can see it. See [Employees](/en/docs/people/employees).

## Government tenders

Pull procurement notices from the Comptroller General's e-GP system as sales leads. See [Government tenders](/en/docs/crm/egp-tenders).
