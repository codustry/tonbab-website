These docs explain how to use Tonbab step by step, from signing up and setting up your company to the everyday work in each module. Menu names and buttons in **bold** are the exact words you will see on screen.

## Where to start

1. [Sign up and set up your company](/en/docs/getting-started/sign-up-and-company)
2. [Add users and set permissions](/en/docs/getting-started/users-roles-permissions)
3. [Move your data from your current software](/en/docs/migration/migrate-from-other-software)

Then open the section that matches your work. Purchasing teams can start with [purchase requests](/en/docs/operation/purchase-requests), shops with [POS](/en/docs/commerce/pos), and HR with [employees](/en/docs/people/employees).
