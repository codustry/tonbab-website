The pack station (the **Pack** app) is used in the warehouse to pack goods against a [delivery note](/en/docs/operation/delivery-notes). Staff scan each item, Tonbab checks it's complete and correct, and the office then issues the delivery note to take the stock out.

## Prepare the device

1. Pair a tablet or Sunmi device as a kiosk with the **Packing** mini-app, as in [Install the app and kiosk mode](/en/docs/getting-started/install-app).
2. Create PINs for packing staff under **POS Operators**.
3. On a Sunmi device, install Sunmi Browser or Sunmi Cloud Print Service to print on the built-in printer.

## Pack a delivery note

1. Sign in with your PIN. The screen shows **Ready to scan**.
2. Scan the delivery note number, or tap **Pick Delivery Note from list**.
3. Tap **Start packing**. Stock is reserved from this device's warehouse.
4. Scan items one by one. On each line you can:
   - **Undo one** after an extra scan
   - **Set lot** for lot-tracked products
   - **Pack the rest** to count the remaining quantity
   - **Skip**, with a reason such as out of stock, damaged, customer request or ship later
5. Tap **Close session**, enter **Boxes** and the total weight, then tap **Confirm pack**.

The delivery note becomes **Packed**. The office then clicks **Issue & Ship** on a computer to take the stock out.

## Other actions

- **Reprint pack slip** if a slip is lost or didn't print.
- **Void** cancels a pack that isn't finished.
- **Request reversal** on a delivery note that was already issued. The request goes to the office for approval.

## Stock tab

The **Stock** tab lets you **Look up stock** in the warehouse, showing on hand, reserved and available quantities, and print a stock slip.

## Printing

The app prints slips on a Sunmi built-in printer or through the browser's print dialog. If nothing prints, check that Sunmi Cloud Print Service is installed and set as the device's default print service.
