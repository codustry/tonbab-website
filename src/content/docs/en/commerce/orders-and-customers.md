The Commerce app keeps orders from every channel, both POS and marketplaces, under **Orders**. Buyer details are under **Customers**, and daily sales are in the **Day report**.

## The orders page

Go to **Commerce** › **Selling** › **Orders**. Each order has **Details** and **History** tabs.

Order statuses: Draft, Placed, Paid, Fulfilled, Completed, Cancelled and Refunded.

On an order you can:

- **Send payment link**: emails the customer a payment link that works for 24 hours.
- **Issue tax invoice** for customers who need one.
- Cancel the order (not possible once shipped).

Recording cash or manual payments is done in [POS](/en/docs/commerce/pos).

## Stock reservation

When an order becomes paid, Tonbab reserves the stock automatically and shows a **Reserved** badge. Stock isn't taken out until you ship.

## Ship an order

1. On the order, click **Create shipment**.
2. The shipment starts at **To pick**. Pick the goods, then start packing.
3. When packed, mark it ready to ship or ready for pickup.
4. Click **Mark shipped**. Stock is issued at this point.

For Shopee or Lazada orders, Tonbab also notifies the marketplace. See [Sales channels](/en/docs/commerce/sales-channels). You can cancel or reopen a shipment if needed.

## Customers

Go to **Commerce** › **Selling** › **Customers**.

- Add a customer with name, type (person or company), phone, email, tax ID and loyalty membership.
- The **Insights** tab summarises buying behaviour.
- The activity tab logs call, email or message notes.
- The orders tab lists every order from that customer.

Customers attached at the POS or linked from chat appear here too.

### Commerce customers and CRM customers

Commerce customers are individual buyers, such as walk-in or online shoppers. B2B business accounts with deals and visits live in [CRM](/en/docs/crm/accounts-contacts-deals). Both use the same company and contact records.

## Day report

The **Day report** shows each day's sales. Step to the previous or next day and filter by channel or store.

- totals by payment
- totals by channel
- dine-in vs take-away
- totals by hour

Download it with **Export CSV**.

## Commerce home

The Commerce **Home** page shows key numbers such as recent sales and order counts, a good place to start the day.
