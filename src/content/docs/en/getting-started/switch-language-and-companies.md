Tonbab works in Thai and English, and each person picks their own language. If you belong to several companies, you can switch between them from the sidebar without signing out.

## Change the language

1. Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar to open the user menu.
2. On the **Language** row, choose TH or EN.

The language is your personal setting and doesn't affect anyone else. Menus, buttons and messages change right away.

### Document language

The screen language and the language printed on documents are separate. Some documents, such as accounting sales documents, have their own **Language** option, so you can issue an English document to a foreign customer even while your screen is in Thai.

### Bilingual names

Data people type in, such as employee names, has separate Thai and English fields. See [Employees](/en/docs/people/employees).

## Switch companies

If your login belongs to more than one company (for example a parent company and a subsidiary):

1. Click the company name at the top of the sidebar.
2. The **Workspaces** list shows every company you can access.
3. Pick the company you want.

Each company's data is completely separate, and your permissions can differ between companies. Running several companies under one contract is part of the Enterprise plan.

## Switch apps

Click **Apps** in the top bar to see the modules your company uses, such as Operation, Commerce, CRM, People, Service, Docs and Accounting. Each app has its own sidebar.

## Search everything

Press **⌘K** (Mac), **Ctrl+K** (Windows) or **/** to open search.

- Search by document number, name, SKU, barcode, tax ID or phone.
- Type po: pr: inv: cus: sku: first to search one kind of record.
- Start with > to jump to a page or run an action.
- Press Tab to switch scope between All, Documents, Contacts, Products, and Pages & actions.

## Collapse the sidebar

Press **⌘B** (or Ctrl+B) to shrink the sidebar to icons, and again to expand it.
