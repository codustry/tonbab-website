You can sign up for Tonbab free at [app.tonbab.com/signup](https://app.tonbab.com/signup). It takes about 3 minutes. Every new company gets a **30-day Business trial · no card needed**. When the trial ends, the company moves to the Free plan automatically, and your data stays.

## How to sign up

The **Open your company on Tonbab** page has 3 steps.

### 1. Account

- Enter **Your name**, **Work email** and **Password** (at least 8 characters).
- Or click **Continue with Google** / **Continue with Microsoft** to use an existing work account.

### 2. Company

These details are printed on your quotations, purchase orders and tax invoices, so use your registered details.

- **Company name (as your team calls it)** and **Company ID**. The ID is used in links and **can't be changed later**.
- **Business type**, for example company limited, limited partnership or individual / sole trader.
- **Registered name in Thai**, and in English (optional).
- **Tax ID (13 digits)**.
- **Company registration date**, as on your DBD certificate. Your books start on this date.
- **Branch** (head office or a 5-digit branch number) and **VAT registered?**

### 3. Address & apps

- Address, province, postal code, company phone and the company email shown on documents.
- **SKU prefix**: 2–5 capital letters. For example, HOS gives product codes like HOS-0001.
- Pick at least one module: Operations, Accounting, Sales & POS, CRM, People, Service and Docs. Accounting is included from the Starter plan up (and during the Business trial).
- Accept the Terms and Privacy Policy, then click **Create company**.

### 4. Confirm your email

Tonbab emails you a confirmation link. Open it to create the company. The link works once. If nothing arrives within a few minutes, check spam, or sign up again to get a new link. (If you signed up with Google or Microsoft, there's no email to confirm.)

## After you sign up

1. Check your company details and upload your logo in **Company Settings** (**Company Details** and **Company Logo**).
2. [Add users and give them roles](/en/docs/getting-started/users-roles-permissions).
3. Import products, business partners and opening balances using [Move your data from your current software](/en/docs/migration/migrate-from-other-software).

## Common questions

**What happens after the 30-day trial?** The company moves to the Free plan. All your data stays, but Free plan limits apply, such as 1 user. See [Plans and billing](/en/docs/getting-started/plans-and-billing).

**What if I enter the wrong registration date?** Use the date on your DBD certificate, because accounting creates your first fiscal year from it. To correct it, edit it in **Company Settings** before you start posting to the books.

**Can I turn on more modules later?** Yes. Contact us at sales@codustry.com or 02-873-4448.
