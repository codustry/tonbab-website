Tonbab works in any web browser at [app.tonbab.com](https://app.tonbab.com), and you can install it on a phone or tablet home screen so it opens full-screen like any other app. For tablets shared at a shop counter or in a warehouse, use **kiosk mode** so staff sign in with a PIN instead of a password.

## Add Tonbab to your home screen

**iPhone / iPad (Safari)**

1. Open app.tonbab.com and sign in.
2. Tap Share.
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**.

**Android (Chrome)**

1. Open app.tonbab.com and sign in.
2. Tap the ⋮ menu.
3. Choose **Install app**.

Tonbab then has its own icon on the home screen and opens full-screen without the browser bar.

## Station apps (kiosks)

The **Kiosks** page lists three apps for tablets and shared devices:

| App | What it's for |
|---|---|
| POS | Retail point of sale |
| Pack | Warehouse pack station |
| Manufacture | Shop-floor material issue |

On the station's tablet, click **Open** on the app you need, then add it to the home screen as above.

## Set up a tablet in kiosk mode

Pair a shared tablet with your company once, then staff sign in with a 4–6 digit PIN.

1. On a computer, go to **Commerce Settings** › **POS** › **Kiosk Devices** and click **Add a device**.
2. Name the device, pick a **Warehouse**, choose the **Mini-apps** it may run (POS, Packing, Manufacturing), and optionally a **Shop** to lock it to one shop.
3. Click **Create device**. Tonbab shows a **Pairing code — shown once**. It expires in 48 hours.
4. On the tablet, open app.tonbab.com/kiosk/pair, enter the code and tap **Pair device**.
5. Create staff PINs under **POS Operators** › **Create operator**.

Staff who sign in with a PIN **don't count as users** on your plan. After 5 wrong PINs, that operator is locked for 30 seconds.

## Leave kiosk mode

A paired device stays in kiosk mode until a manager approves leaving it. Go to **Leave kiosk mode**, pick the manager and enter their PIN. (A manager is an operator with **Allow to approve refunds** switched on.) A kiosk admin can also use their email and password instead.

## Printers

POS and the pack station print on Sunmi devices with a built-in printer, or on network receipt printers. See [POS](/en/docs/commerce/pos) and [Pack station](/en/docs/commerce/pack-station).
